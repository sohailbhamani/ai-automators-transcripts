# Think Your RAG Agent is Hallucinating? You Might Be Wrong

So, your rag agent gives you a wrong answer. You might assume that it's hallucinating, but you could be diagnosing the wrong problem. Hallucinations are when an AI model makes things up. Whereas faithfulness means that it's trusting bad data coming from the knowledge base. Maybe the documents retrieved from the vector store had errors or chunking cut off some crucial context. In this case, the AI is actually being faithful to the bad data. So, it's important that you test faithfulness separately from accuracy.
