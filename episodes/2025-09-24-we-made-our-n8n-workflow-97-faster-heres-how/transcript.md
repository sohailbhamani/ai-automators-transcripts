# 🚀 We Made Our n8n Workflow 97% FASTER - Here's How

So, your N8 workflow is slow. Here's how you find the real bottlenecks. Step one, check your execution logs. Click logs at the bottom, then click overview and look for the big numbers. We found nodes that were taking more than 4 seconds when they should be less than a second. Step two, don't assume adding more resources will make this faster. We found actually adding workers made it slower because there was a different bottleneck we needed to solve. Step three, sometimes you need to ditch the official APIs and go direct to source. When scaling up this system, we ditched Google Drive for a direct SFTP connection. And the result, 97% faster performance with parallel processing of files. So you really need to tune and scale your workflow systematically.
