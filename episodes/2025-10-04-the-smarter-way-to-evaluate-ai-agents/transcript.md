# The SMARTER Way to Evaluate AI Agents

When you're testing AI agents, you should think of it like a basketball court. Green shots are correct answers. Red shots are misses. But here's what most people miss. The boundaries are more important than the shots. Your agent could answer, "Who is the 29th president of the United States?" perfectly. But if you're building an e-commerce bot, that's both irrelevant and out of bounds. The pros don't waste time on evaluating answers for questions that are out of bounds. They focus on mapping every corner of the court first.
