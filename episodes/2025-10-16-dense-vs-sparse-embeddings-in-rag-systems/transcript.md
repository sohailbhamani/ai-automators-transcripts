# Dense vs Sparse Embeddings in RAG Systems

Which retrieval approach is better for your AI agent? Vector embeddings or keyword search? While dense vector embeddings understand that the word car is also related to vehicle and automobile. So, it's great for semantic style search, but it can fail miserably when it's looking for the likes of product codes or ids or words that are not well represented in its training data. Sparse retrieval or keyword search on the other hand extracts out tokens from the words that it ingests and then it's looking for exact matches on those tokens. So if you're building a rag agent, you most likely need both. Semantic search for understanding concepts and lexical search or keyword search for exact precise matches. This is why hybrid search usually isn't optional. It's essential in production rag systems.
