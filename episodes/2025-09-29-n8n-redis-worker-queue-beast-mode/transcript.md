# n8n + Redis 🔥Worker Queue BEAST Mode

We just spent 100 hours scaling and tuning our AI workflow and achieved a 97% performance improvement. Originally, we could only process a 100 files an hour. Now, we're up to 5,000. That's 10 gigs of data with quarter of a million chunks in our vector store. And the biggest win was proper worker cues in Reddus. Four workers each handling 10 tasks concurrently gave us bandwidth to process 40 files at a time. So end to end we jumped from 26 seconds per file to 0.7 seconds.
