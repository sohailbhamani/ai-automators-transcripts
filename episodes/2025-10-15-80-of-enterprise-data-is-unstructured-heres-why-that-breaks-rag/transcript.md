# 80% of Enterprise Data is Unstructured - Here's Why That Breaks RAG

80 to 90% of all data in enterprise is in unstructured formats. We're talking about PDFs, word documents, emails, meeting transcripts. And this by and large can be very messy data. And with rag systems, it's garbage in garbage out. So it's crucial that you actually pre-process your data by using techniques like OCR and metadata extraction to represent that data cleanly in your vector stores. This is the difference between a clean proof of concept versus the messy reality of a production rag
